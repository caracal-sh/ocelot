- No more stray window while the agent works. When the agent took a screenshot, the screen being rendered could appear on your desktop behind your other windows. Rendering now happens in a window you can never see.
- Shorter handoff prompts. Links that many screens share, like a sidebar, are listed once for all of them instead of once per screen, so a large app's handoff prompt is about a tenth of its old size and your coding agent spends its context on the design instead.
